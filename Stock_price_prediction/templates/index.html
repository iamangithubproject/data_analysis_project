How This Analysis Works
  • RSI(14) — a 14-period Relative Strength Index is calculated from daily closes. RSI < 30 → BUY (oversold), RSI > 70 → SELL (overbought), otherwise HOLD.
  • Next-close forecast — a straight line is fitted through the last 30 closing prices and projected one trading day forward.
  • Explainable, not magic — the "AI" interface is driven by these transparent rules; no trained neural network is involved.
  • Data — approximately one year of daily OHLCV history from Yahoo Finance, cached for 5 minutes.
Disclaimer: this dashboard is for learning and demonstration only. Nothing here is financial advice — trend-based forecasts are estimates, not guarantees.
